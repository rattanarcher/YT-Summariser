## Overview

This video from Tempo's "Bocor Alus Politik" examines the extraordinary influence wielded by Lieutenant Colonel Teddy Indra Wijaya, Indonesia's Cabinet Secretary under President Prabowo Subianto. The journalists Raymond Serikang, Husein Abri Dongoran, and Francisca Kristi Rosana investigate how Teddy has become the primary gatekeeper to President Prabowo, with ministers reporting directly to him rather than through traditional channels. The discussion reveals concerns about disrupted governmental hierarchy and accelerated military career advancement that appears to bypass established merit systems within the Indonesian Armed Forces (TNI).

## Key Highlights

The investigation reveals that Teddy Indra Wijaya has assumed an unprecedented role as the central coordinator for ministerial access to President Prabowo. Ministers including Social Affairs Minister Saifullah Yusuf and Housing Minister Maruarar Sirait regularly report their program progress to Teddy, often weekly, rather than following traditional bureaucratic channels through coordinating ministers. This pattern extends beyond domestic officials to foreign diplomatic representatives, who complain they must go through Teddy to reach the President. The journalists discovered that ministers deemed close to Teddy receive priority access to the President, while those outside his circle face difficulties securing meetings. Some ministers on reshuffling lists have notably begun courting Teddy's favor through social media posts and direct engagement.

The program also exposes significant concerns within military circles about Teddy's rapid career advancement. Despite graduating from the Military Academy in 2011, Teddy achieved Lieutenant Colonel rank in just 14 years, compared to the standard 18-year requirement. Military officers, both active and retired, express frustration that the TNI Commander changed promotion regulations in March 2025, reducing the minimum service period from 18 to 14 years, seemingly tailored to legitimize Teddy's advancement. Sources suggest preparations are underway for Teddy to achieve general rank by 2029, with a senior retired general reportedly tasked by President Prabowo to design Teddy's pathway to one-star general status within six years.

The journalists trace Teddy's rise from his initial appointment as President Jokowi's aide in a program designed to bring young TNI officers into the palace, through his specialized training including Ranger School in the United States, to his eventual selection as Prabowo's aide and subsequently Cabinet Secretary. His media presence, managed by the same team that handled Jokowi's social media, positions him as a central figure rather than merely highlighting presidential programs, contrasting with the approach used for other officials.

## Implications

This concentration of power in the hands of a single Cabinet Secretary represents a significant departure from established governmental procedures and could undermine Indonesia's institutional checks and balances. The apparent manipulation of military promotion standards to accommodate one individual's career trajectory raises serious questions about merit-based advancement within the TNI and could demoralize professional military officers who have followed traditional pathways.
