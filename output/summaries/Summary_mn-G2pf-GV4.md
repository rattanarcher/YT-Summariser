## Overview
Indonesian President Prabowo Subianto conducted another cabinet reshuffle, marking his fourth or fifth cabinet restructuring within the first year of his administration. The reshuffle focused primarily on strengthening coordination and communication within the Red and White Cabinet, with key appointments including new positions in the Presidential Staff Office (KSP) and communications advisory roles. LSI Executive Director Jayadi Hanan analyzed this latest reshuffle as addressing internal coordination problems but noted that critical economic challenges remain unaddressed.

## Key Highlights

The latest cabinet reshuffle reveals the Prabowo administration's ongoing struggle with internal coordination and communication issues. According to Jayadi Hanan from the Indonesian Survey Institute (LSI), this restructuring primarily strengthens the Presidential Staff Office's role in coordinating priority government programs and managing strategic issues, while also reinforcing the government's communications apparatus through new advisory positions. The reshuffle brought back several figures previously considered close to former President Jokowi, including Abdul Kadir Karding and Hasan Nasbi, suggesting efforts to maintain political coalition solidarity rather than purely performance-based decisions.

However, Hanan criticized the administration for failing to address more pressing economic challenges that require immediate attention. With rising fuel prices, increasing costs of basic goods, and economic pressures from ongoing Middle Eastern conflicts affecting Indonesia's economy, the reshuffle notably avoided restructuring the economic team. Instead of streamlining what is already considered an oversized cabinet with 48 ministries, Prabowo added new deputy ministerial positions, potentially exacerbating rather than solving coordination problems. The repeated reshuffles suggest underlying management and leadership issues rather than political friction, given the coalition's overwhelming 80% parliamentary support.

The LSI analysis points to fundamental problems in the administration's approach to governance and public engagement. Hanan emphasized that the government needs to shift its paradigm regarding criticism, viewing critical voices as constructive rather than destructive forces. The focus on communication improvements suggests recognition of these issues, but the failure to address substantive policy challenges in economic management, job creation, and price control indicates that structural problems may persist despite cosmetic changes in cabinet composition.

## Implications
The repeated cabinet reshuffles signal potential instability and management challenges within the Prabowo administration, despite its strong political coalition. The failure to address economic policy teams amid fiscal pressures and global economic challenges could undermine public confidence and economic recovery efforts. The administration's approach to handling criticism and internal coordination will be crucial for maintaining governance effectiveness and public support throughout the remaining tenure.
